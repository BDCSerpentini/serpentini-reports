# Automations & Broadcasts tab

Replace `index.html` in your existing GitHub Pages `serpentini-reports-main` directory. Do not delete or overwrite your existing `reports.json`—it contains previously published month-level reports. The website tab includes October 2026 outreach reports as fallback. When future Automations or Broadcasts reports are published from Streamlit, the site reads them from `reports.json`.

Views: Overview, Automations, Broadcasts. All use global year/quarter/month/dealership filters. Comparison charts automatically switch between bars for counts and dots/lollipops for rates, with sorting, hover details, consistent dealership colors, trend chart and report detail tables. Missing dealership data are not zero. The October per-dealer exports include six Chevrolet locations; no CDJR rows.
